### Phase 12 contract

- `streetMood="auto"` resolves from `streetHour` through the frozen `HOUR_TO_MOOD` mapping.

- Manual moods including `dust`, `souq`, `event`, and fixed time-of-day moods override the hour mapping.
- `street_mood` is reserved from generic scene-fact rendering and is consumed only by `describeSaudiStreetRealism()`.

- Hour 21 resolves to `cafe` and emits exactly three evening cafe-street details.
- Golden prompts remain at or below **250 words** because legacy golden inputs contain no new mood field and retain the Phase 10 fallback.

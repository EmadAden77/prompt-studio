# Phase 28 — Saudi realism modifiers

Phase 28 adds deterministic, brand-safe Saudi environmental detail on top of the Phase 10–12 mood/hour system.

- Automatic street moods select daylight or night realism from the resolved lighting source.
- Manual dust, souq, and event moods retain their specialized existing behavior.
- Grocery and barbershop scenes receive their matching local-shop details.
- Each active scene emits at most one place sentence and one soft-focus background-human sentence.
- Storefront references use generic, unreadable background graphics with no brand names or generated lettering.
- The adapter remains read-only and deterministic while budget protection keeps every tested prompt at 250 words or fewer.
